New to eSIM? What it is and how to use it

An eSIM is a digital SIM you install on a compatible phone, without swapping your physical SIM card. Travel eSIMs provide mobile data; calls and texts depend on the plan.

  1. Check your device. Confirm your exact phone model supports eSIM and is network-unlocked. Some regional models do not support it.
  2. Choose your destination. Review coverage, data allowance, validity and the activation notes on each plan before paying.
  3. Install over Wi-Fi. Follow the QR code or manual setup instructions we send you. Turn on the eSIM for mobile data and enable data roaming if instructed.

Do not delete an installed eSIM unless the instructions tell you to. Reinstallation may not be supported.
