New to eSIM? What it is and how to use it

An eSIM is a digital SIM you install on a compatible phone, without swapping your physical SIM card. Travel eSIMs typically provide mobile data; calls and texts depend on the plan.

  1. Check your device. Confirm your exact phone model supports eSIM and is network-unlocked. Some regional models do not support it.
  2. Choose your destination. When live plans launch, review coverage, data allowance, validity and activation rules before paying.
  3. Install over Wi-Fi. Follow the provider’s QR code or manual setup instructions. Turn on the eSIM for mobile data and enable data roaming if instructed.

Do not delete an installed eSIM unless the provider tells you to. Reinstallation may not be supported.
